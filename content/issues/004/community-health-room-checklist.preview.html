When money, power or supplies are constrained

  • No new filters or masks. Adaptation: pool community supplies, reduce crowding, offer remote options, and consider EPA-guided DIY cleaning when a commercial cleaner is unavailable. Safety boundary: do not claim untested devices or dirty filters perform as rated.
  • Electrical outage. Adaptation: if outdoor conditions and accessibility permit, meet outside; otherwise postpone or go asynchronous. Safety boundary: an unplugged cleaner does nothing; never run fuel generators indoors or endanger medical-device power.
  • Poor outdoor air or severe weather. Adaptation: maintain safe building air handling, postpone, or change format. Safety boundary: opening windows may be harmful during smoke, pollution, or temperature extremes.
  • Illness circulating. Adaptation: support staying home without punishment, reduce indoor exposure, and help deliver essentials. Safety boundary: no informal medical registry or pressure to reveal test results.
  • Accessibility gaps. Adaptation: change schedule, location, transport, format, lighting, seating, or activity demands. Safety boundary: "Everyone is welcome" is not evidence everyone can attend.

Reading and verification

  • CDC: Cleaner air — ventilation, outdoor meetings, HEPA filtration, and the need to use actual building capacity.
  • CDC: Respirator and mask protection — fit and type matter.
  • CDC: Prevention when sick — staying home and added precautions, including organization policies.
  • EPA: Air cleaners and respiratory viruses — tested commercial devices are preferable for routine use when feasible, but well-built DIY cleaners may be effective when commercial devices are unavailable; build and operate safely.
  • CDC: Long COVID clinical guidance and ME/CFS post-exertional malaise guidance — limitations, validation, individualized pacing.

Scope: A template for an operator's actual room, not a claim the 561 Group has deployed, measured, certified or staffed a health facility. It requires local hazard, accessibility and scientific review before being represented as a tested intervention.
